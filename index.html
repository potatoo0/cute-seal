01 / little introduction

Soft body.
Big personality.

Seals are marine mammals built for life in the water, with streamlined bodies, sensitive whiskers, and an impressive ability to hold their breath while diving.

They also have the important job of being ridiculously cute. Science has not officially assigned this role, but the evidence is overwhelming.

Whisker radar

A seal's whiskers can detect tiny movements in the water, helping it find prey even when visibility is poor.

🌊

Built to dive

Seals can make impressive dives by slowing their heart rate and managing how their bodies use oxygen underwater.

🫧

Underwater pros

Their streamlined shape and powerful flippers help them move efficiently through the water.
